Privacy Policy

Tile Path Twist — by Daynix Studio
Last updated: October 9, 2026
Tile Path Twist is a fully offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Nothing you do inside the game ever leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. Tile Path Twist does not require an account, does not ask for permissions, and does not read anything from your phone. There is no sign-in, no email prompt, and no profile to create.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The game saves your gameplay progress locally on your device using standard app storage. This is used solely to let you resume where you left off and includes:

  • Completed levels and star ratings
  • Best move counts per level
  • Total hints and undos used
  • Unlocked achievements
  • Daily puzzle results
  • Your theme preference (Light or Dark)

All of this data stays on your device. We cannot see it, we cannot access it, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

3. Internet Access

Tile Path Twist does not require an internet connection. The game runs entirely offline and does not communicate with any server, service, or third party.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The app contains no advertisements, no third-party advertising networks, and no analytics or crash reporting services. We do not use any tracking tools of any kind.

5. Permissions

Tile Path Twist requests no special permissions on your device. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, photos, or any other sensitive data.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it is safe for use by children. No account creation is required, no data is uploaded, and there is no social interaction, chat, or user content of any kind.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Since the game stays fully offline and collects nothing, future updates will continue to respect your privacy by default.

8. Deleting Your Data

To delete all locally stored game data, open the Settings screen inside Tile Path Twist and tap Reset Progress. This clears all levels, stars, achievements, and daily history from your device. Alternatively, uninstalling the app will remove all data as well.
